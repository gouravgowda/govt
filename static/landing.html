How it works

Three steps. No paperwork.

Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored beyond your answers, and nothing is shared with any authority.

1

Answer 8 plain questions

Age, state, work stage, income band — type in your own words or pick a suggestion. Anything you'd rather not share, type skip.

2

See your ranked schemes

Eligible schemes first, each with a written reason and a match score. Near-misses show exactly what gap stands between you and the scheme.

3

Apply via official links

We link only to government websites and list the documents to carry. You apply yourself — we never stand between you and the authority.

Built to be safe

Guardrails, stated plainly.

A matching tool must earn trust before it earns clicks. These are our rules.

Official sources only

Each scheme entry carries its government URL and the date we last verified it.

No sensitive documents

No Aadhaar, PAN or bank details are asked for, at any point, for any reason.

Transparent ranking

Every result states why it ranked where it did — in one sentence you can check.

Guidance, not a verdict

We indicate eligibility; the scheme authority decides. Stale entries are flagged.
